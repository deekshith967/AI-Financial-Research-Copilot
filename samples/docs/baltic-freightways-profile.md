# Baltic Freightways AB — Company Profile (Fictional Sample)

Fictional sample document for retrieval pipeline testing. Baltic Freightways
AB (ticker BLTF) is not a real company and all figures are invented.

## Fleet and Operations

Baltic Freightways AB operates a fleet of 14 ice-class dry bulk vessels
serving Northern European and Arctic trade routes. The fleet's average age
is 7 years, and five vessels are fitted with scrubber systems for
high-sulphur fuel compliance.

The company generates revenue from time charters and spot voyages, with a
charter backlog covering approximately 55 percent of available ship days for
the coming year.

## Dividend Policy

The board has adopted a dividend policy targeting a payout ratio of 40
percent of net income, distributed semi-annually. For the last fiscal year
the company paid a total dividend of 1.85 euros per share.

## Risk Notes

Bunker fuel costs are partially hedged: about half of expected fuel
consumption for the next twelve months is covered by fixed-price swaps.
Ice conditions during severe winters can delay Arctic voyages and raise
insurance premiums.

Dry bulk rates are cyclical; a downturn in iron ore and grain shipments
would reduce spot voyage revenue and pressure the charter renewal pipeline.
