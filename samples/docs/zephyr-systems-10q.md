# Zephyr Systems Inc. — Q3 FY2026 Quarterly Update (Fictional Sample)

Inc. (ticker ZPHY) is not a real company and all figures are invented.

## Results Overview

Revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 was 118 million dollars, up 11
percent year over year. Stratoserve annual recurring revenue reached 260
million dollars at quarter end.

Utilisation across the nine operating sites averaged 87 percent. The first
Iceland site began limited commissioning in February 2026 and is expected to
reach full capacity in the fourth quarter.

## Cost Developments

A helium surcharge of 4 percent was applied to on-demand burst pricing during
the quarter in response to higher industrial helium procurement costs.
Reserved contracts were not affected by the surcharge.

Electricity hedges fixed approximately 60 percent of expected FY2026 power
consumption at contracted rates.

## Outlook

Management reiterated full-year FY2026 revenue guidance of 470 to 485 million
dollars and highlighted ramp risk at the Iceland site as the main variable.
