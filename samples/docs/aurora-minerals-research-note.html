Investment Summary

We initiate coverage of Aurora Minerals Ltd with a constructive view. The company's Andes-4 lithium brine project in northern Chile is the core asset, with expected nameplate capacity of 25,000 tonnes of lithium carbonate equivalent per year.

Total project capex is estimated at 340 million dollars, with first production targeted for early 2028. Our twelve-month price target implies upside driven by Andes-4 de-risking milestones rather than near-term earnings.

Key Risks

Brine grade variability at Andes-4 could reduce recovery rates relative to pilot-plant results. Water usage permits in the Atacama region remain subject to community consultation and could tighten operating conditions.

Lithium prices are volatile; a prolonged period below 12,000 dollars per tonne would compress project economics and delay the expansion decision.

Financial Position

Aurora ended the last reported quarter with 96 million dollars in cash and no drawn debt. Management expects to fund Andes-4 through a mix of cash on hand, a planned streaming arrangement, and equipment finance.
